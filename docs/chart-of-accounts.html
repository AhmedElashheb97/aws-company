Chart of Accounts

Proposal for AWS COMPANY · car import office
Prepared November 2026

How the office works (basis for this chart): cars are bought at US auctions (Copart, IAAI) for customers and shipped to Al-Khoms, Libya. Customers always pay the office; the office pays the auction and the shipping companies (Atlantic Express, MTL). The office earns a commission, a 1.5% transfer commission, and a margin on inland towing and ocean freight. There are no office overheads (no rent, salaries, electricity or internet).

Each account shows where its numbers already come from in the office app, or that it does not exist there yet.

Pass-through money is neither revenue nor expense. The car’s auction price and auction fees, and any costs charged to the customer (fines, storage, FedEx, relocation to another port), are collected from the customer and paid on to the auction or the shipping company. They move between 12 Customer receivables and 11 Cash and never touch accounts 4 or 5. The app already treats them this way.

In short: the app fully covers the customer and profit side. What is missing is the office’s own money side — where the cash is, what the office owes others, and what the owner has taken out.

Suggested first step if this is built: add only 11 Cash accounts, 21 Payable to shipping companies and 32 Owner’s drawings. Together they answer “how much money should I have right now?”, and the cash can be checked against it at the end of each month. Everything else can wait until the business needs it.
